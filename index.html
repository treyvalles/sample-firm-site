About the owner

A licensed CPA with more than fifteen years in practice, first inside larger firms and then on their own. The firm stays small on purpose: fewer clients means the person you hire is the person who knows your numbers, returns your call, and remembers the context behind last year's return.

Plainspoken about money, allergic to jargon, and genuinely happy when a client stops dreading tax season.
